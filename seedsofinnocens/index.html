Stage 1 · Channel Setup & Discoverability
Before content quality matters, the channel's basic configuration determines whether YouTube's algorithm can index, categorise, and recommend it. 6 of 10 setup elements are broken or missing.
✗ Critical
Subscribers below 1,000 — count hidden
YouTube hides counts below 1,000. No monetisation eligibility, and the recommendation engine suppresses reach for channels without social proof signals. The channel cannot compound algorithmically.
✗ Critical
Brand name inconsistency in bio
Handle is @seedsofinnocens but bio reads "Seeds of Innocence" — two spellings on the same page. Google's entity recognition reads these as separate brands, splitting YouTube and web SEO authority.
✗ Critical
No playlist architecture
Videos sit in a flat, uncategorised list. Without playlists, YouTube cannot identify the channel as an authority in any topic cluster — and watch time cannot compound between related videos.
✗ Critical
Stale channel description
Bio says "15,000 successful cycles." The current claim is 20,000+ deliveries. Outdated bio signals an inactive channel to both algorithm and new visitors. No keywords are properly formatted.
✗ Issue
Malformed channel keywords
Tags like "Bestivfcentre" and "InfertilityClinic" (no spaces, no separators) are parsed as single unknown terms by YouTube's keyword engine. All keyword discoverability value is lost.
✗ Issue
No channel trailer or featured video
New visitors land on a blank channel with no introduction. A 60-second trailer is the highest-converting single element for new visitor-to-subscriber conversion — and it is absent.
△ Warning
No clickable links in description
YouTube allows 5 links in channel bio. All 5 slots are unused. A phone number is listed as plain text — not tappable. Book Appointment, WhatsApp, and website links are missing entirely.
