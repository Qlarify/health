## Hyderabad's hospital market overview

Hyderabad has one of India's most active private hospital markets, anchored by KIMS Hospitals (one of the most recognised multi-specialty groups in South India), Apollo Hospitals, Yashoda Hospitals, Care Hospitals, Continental Hospitals, and MaxCure Hospitals.

The city's healthcare market is growing on the back of a rapidly expanding IT economy, a large and relatively young population, and improving health insurance penetration in Telangana. New hospital campuses are opening regularly across the Gachibowli-Madhapur corridor, Banjara Hills, and Kukatpally.

Three characteristics define Hyderabad's hospital video market:

**Telugu-language patient base.** The majority of Hyderabad's residential patient base is Telugu-speaking. Yet almost no hospital in the city produces patient-facing video content in Telugu. Patient education videos, OPD campaign content, and discharge guides in Telugu are entirely absent from most Hyderabad hospital video libraries.

**No dedicated hospital video specialist.** Unlike Bengaluru (where at least one general agency has built a healthcare vertical), Hyderabad has no agency that positions itself specifically as a hospital video production specialist. The agencies currently ranking for hospital video production in Hyderabad serve technology companies and consumer brands alongside healthcare.

**High decision-stage gap.** Most Hyderabad hospitals that produce video content produce it almost entirely at the Trust Building stage — doctor introduction videos and facility tours. The Decision stage (procedure explainers, patient testimonials) and Post-Operative Care stage (discharge guides, recovery videos) are almost entirely absent, in both English and Telugu.

## Why Telugu-language hospital videos are underserved

Telugu is the primary language of approximately 75% of Hyderabad's residential patient base. Yet the overwhelming majority of hospital video content in the city is produced only in English — occasionally with Hindi voiceover — with no Telugu production at all.

The reasons are structural rather than strategic. General video agencies don't have Telugu medical voiceover capability, Telugu medical scriptwriters, or the cultural familiarity to write patient-facing health content in Telugu that meets both clinical accuracy and NMC compliance standards.

The opportunity this creates for Hyderabad hospitals is significant:

A patient education video in Telugu about when to see a cardiologist — published on a hospital's YouTube channel with Telugu metadata — faces zero competition from any other Hyderabad hospital. A Meta campaign video in Telugu for a diabetes OPD awareness campaign targets a patient segment that no other hospital in the city is reaching with language-matched creative.

For hospitals like KIMS that have large campuses across Hyderabad and a primarily Telugu-speaking patient base, building a Telugu-language video library is the single fastest-to-differentiate opportunity in the city's hospital marketing landscape.

## What to look for in a Hyderabad hospital video agency

The absence of a dedicated hospital video specialist in Hyderabad means most agencies you will encounter are general video production companies. Here is how to evaluate them:

**Hospital-only positioning.** The agency should work exclusively with hospitals, not serve healthcare as one vertical among many. This affects scriptwriting depth, production crew briefing, and compliance workflow.

**NMC and ASCI compliance built in.** Every hospital video script should be reviewed against NMC ethical guidelines and ASCI advertising codes before a camera rolls. Any agency that treats compliance as a post-production check does not understand how regulated healthcare marketing works.

**Telugu production capability.** If the agency cannot produce in Telugu — with native medical voiceover, culturally accurate scripting, and Telangana-appropriate production values — it cannot serve the majority of Hyderabad's patient base.

**Named Hyderabad hospital work.** Can the agency show named hospital client work from Hyderabad or from comparable South Indian hospital markets? National portfolio with no Hyderabad presence often means the agency is quoting for a market it doesn't actually know.

**Patient journey structure.** Does the agency understand that a video strategy must cover all four patient journey stages — Symptom Awareness, Trust Building, Decision, and Post-Operative Care — not just the Trust Building stage that most hospital video libraries over-index on?

## Pricing for hospital video production in Hyderabad

Pricing in Hyderabad's hospital video market is broadly similar to Bengaluru, with slight variations based on production complexity and language requirements:

**Patient testimonial video:** ₹40,000 – ₹80,000. Includes pre-production consultation, scripted interview, shoot at the hospital, post-production, and subtitling in English and Telugu.

**Doctor branding video:** ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000. Includes scripted interview, B-roll at the hospital, branded graphics with credential verification, and compliance review.

**Animated procedure explainer:** ₹80,000 – ₹2,00,000 depending on 2D or 3D production. Includes clinical accuracy review, animation, Telugu and English voiceover.

**OPD awareness campaign (3–5 videos):** ₹60,000 – ₹1,50,000 for the set. Includes scripting, shoot, editing to platform specifications, and compliance review.

**Full specialty library (15–20 videos):** ₹8 – ₹12 lakh. Covers a complete specialty across all four patient journey stages, in English and Telugu.

The absence of a specialist agency in Hyderabad means some general video agencies may quote lower prices — but without NMC compliance review, without Telugu medical scripting, and without patient journey structure. A non-compliant hospital video that needs to be recalled or edited post-publication costs far more than the original production fee.
