# Google Ads for Hospitals in India: The Complete Guide (2026)

Google Ads for hospitals in India is simultaneously the fastest way to generate OPD enquiries and the fastest way to burn through a marketing budget with nothing to show. The difference between the two outcomes is not budget size — it is strategy, structure, and an understanding of how Indian patients search for healthcare.

This guide is written for hospital marketing heads and CMOs who want to understand what a well-run Google Ads account should look like, what benchmarks to hold their agency to, and where most hospital campaigns go wrong.

---

## Why Google Ads Works Differently for Healthcare

Healthcare search intent in India has a distinct pattern that most performance agencies — trained on e-commerce and D2C brands — get wrong.

A patient searching *"best cardiologist in Bengaluru"* is not at the same stage as one searching *"chest pain left side when breathing."* The first is ready to book. The second is scared and needs information. Most hospital Google Ads campaigns only target the first type of query, missing 70–80% of the patient journey.

Effective hospital Google Ads strategy maps to the **four stages of patient decision-making:**

1. **Symptom awareness** — *"chest pain causes," "blood sugar 200 fasting normal?"*
2. **Condition understanding** — *"what is aortic stenosis," "Type 2 diabetes treatment options India"*
3. **Provider evaluation** — *"best heart hospital Bengaluru," "cardiac surgery cost India"*
4. **Decision / booking** — *"book cardiologist appointment online Bengaluru"*

Your Google Ads should run across all four stages — with different ad types, landing pages, and KPIs for each.

## Campaign Types Every Hospital Account Should Run

### Search Campaigns (Non-negotiable)

Search campaigns capture patients actively looking for your specialty or hospital by name. Structure your campaigns by specialty — one campaign per major specialty (Cardiology, Orthopaedics, Neurology, etc.).

Use Phrase Match and Exact Match. Broad Match in healthcare leads to irrelevant clicks from medical students, researchers, and abroad-based users.

Location targeting: the 15–25km radius around your hospital for most specialties; expand to state-level for rare or complex procedures (liver transplant, cancer surgery) where patients travel.

### Performance Max (PMax) — Use With Caution

PMax campaigns run across Google's entire inventory — Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Maps. For hospitals, PMax has one legitimate use case: **brand awareness for new hospital launches or new specialty departments.** For direct patient acquisition, it is hard to control and often over-spends on Display inventory with poor conversion quality.

If your agency is running PMax as your primary campaign type, request a channel-level attribution breakdown. In our experience auditing hospital PMax accounts, a significant portion of attributed "conversions" trace back to Display inventory — and many of those leads do not convert to answered calls. Always verify attribution data before drawing budget conclusions.

### Call-Only Campaigns

For Indian patients, the phone call is still the primary booking mechanism. Call-only campaigns show ads on mobile that, when clicked, directly dial your OPD number — eliminating the form-fill step entirely.

Set up **call conversion tracking** in Google Ads. Without it, you cannot measure which keywords are driving calls vs. just clicks. Google Ads supports call tracking natively (Google forwarding numbers) or via third-party tools.

### Local Search Ads (Google Maps)

For most hospitals, Google Maps is where a significant portion of local patients first encounter you. Ensure your Google Business Profile is verified and fully completed before running Local Search Ads — the ad amplifies your profile's existing signals.

## The Quality Score Problem in Hospital Campaigns

Quality Score (QS) is Google's 1–10 rating of how relevant your ad, keyword, and landing page are to each other. A higher QS means lower CPC for the same ad position.

Most hospital campaigns have low Quality Scores — typically 3–5 out of 10 — because:

**1. Generic landing pages.** An ad for "knee replacement surgery Bengaluru" that sends users to the hospital homepage has a terrible QS. Build specialty-specific landing pages for each campaign.

**2. Keyword-ad-landing page misalignment.** Your ad headline must include the keyword. Your landing page must include the keyword prominently in the H1 and first paragraph.

**3. Slow mobile page speed.** Google penalises slow landing pages. Target sub-2-second load time on 4G.

**4. High bounce rate.** Strong landing pages with clear CTAs — call button, form, WhatsApp link — reduce bounce rates and signal relevance.

**Benchmark:** A well-managed hospital Google Ads account should achieve an average QS of 6–8. Below 5 means your agency is costing you money on every click.

## Hospital-Specific Google Ads Policies

Google has specific restrictions on healthcare advertising in India:

1. **Personal healthcare ads policy.** General hospital services (OPD, surgery, diagnostics) do not require certification. Some categories (clinical trials, certain prescription medications) require prior Google certification.

2. **Sensitive ad categories.** Reproductive health, addiction treatment, and mental health services face additional targeting restrictions — you cannot use Custom Intent audiences based on sensitive health searches.

3. **No misleading claims.** Ads cannot claim "best," "number one," "cure," or make unsubstantiated medical claims. Verifiable accreditations are fine.

4. **Remarketing limitations.** You cannot remarket to users based on health conditions. Build remarketing lists on site visitors generally, not condition-specific pages.

## Landing Page Essentials for Hospital Google Ads

Every specialty campaign needs a dedicated landing page:

- **H1 with specialty and location:** *"Cardiac Sciences — Manipal Hospital, Bengaluru"*
- **Doctor team** with photos, qualifications, and subspecialty — patients choose doctors, not hospitals
- **Key procedures** listed with brief descriptions
- **Social proof** — NABH accreditation, JCI accreditation if applicable, patient outcome data
- **Multiple CTA formats** — click-to-call phone number, WhatsApp click, and short appointment form
- **Mobile-first design** — the majority of Indian healthcare searches happen on mobile

## Benchmarks: What Good Looks Like for Hospital Google Ads in India

| Metric | Poor | Average | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Click-through rate (Search) | below 2% | 3–5% | 6–8% | above 8% |
| CPC — Tier 2/3 cities | above ₹150 | ₹80–150 | ₹50–80 | below ₹50 |
| CPC — Metro, competitive specialty | above ₹500 | ₹300–500 | ₹150–300 | below ₹150 |
| Conversion rate (click to enquiry) | below 2% | 3–5% | 6–10% | above 10% |
| Quality Score (avg) | below 4 | 5–6 | 7–8 | 9–10 |

**Cost per qualified OPD enquiry:** Across Qlarify Health's hospital client portfolio, well-managed accounts deliver ₹128–300 per qualified OPD enquiry depending on specialty and geography. Surgical specialties in competitive metros sit at the higher end; general OPD and health check-up campaigns at the lower end.

**Note on "qualified enquiry":** Define this clearly with your agency. An enquiry is qualified if: the caller is in your catchment area, calling for the right specialty, and is a genuine patient. Call tracking and recording are essential to measure this honestly.

## Meta Ads for Hospitals: The Complement to Google

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads serve a different role than Google Ads in hospital marketing. Google captures **active intent** — patients already searching. Meta creates **latent intent** — patients who were not thinking about their health until they saw your ad. [Hospital social media](/social) and [YouTube video content](/video) feed Meta's retargeting pools — warm video viewers convert at 4–8× the rate of cold traffic.

Meta works best for health awareness campaigns, new service launches, doctor personal branding videos, and remarketing (with DPDP-compliant consent).

The mistake most hospitals make is running Meta ads with the same direct-response creative they use on Google. An educational hook (*"What does chest tightness really mean?"*) followed by a soft CTA consistently outperforms a direct "Book an appointment" ad on Meta.

**Meta CPC benchmarks for India healthcare:** ₹3–25 per click depending on specialty, creative, and audience targeting. Cost per lead: ₹200–600 for health check-up campaigns; ₹400–1,200 for surgical procedure enquiries.

## Common Mistakes in Hospital Google Ads Campaigns

**1. One campaign for all specialties.** When your Cardiology, Orthopaedics, and Gynaecology budgets live in one campaign, Google allocates spend based on what is cheapest to serve — not what is most valuable. Separate campaigns give you control.

**2. No call tracking.** If you cannot prove which keywords drove which phone calls, you are flying blind. Call tracking is ₹3,000–8,000/month and pays for itself within days.

**3. Running ads 24/7.** OPD enquiries happen during business hours. Use ad scheduling to concentrate spend between 8am–8pm.

**4. Ignoring search terms report.** Review weekly. Add irrelevant queries — *"cardiac surgery salary in India," "best medical colleges for cardiology"* — as negative keywords.

**5. No attribution model.** Use Google Analytics 4 with proper UTM tagging across all channels — including [WhatsApp sequences](/email) and [organic search](/seo) so you know which mix is actually driving OPD bookings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What should a hospital's Google Ads budget be in India?**
For a single specialty, a minimum of ₹50,000–₹80,000/month to get statistically meaningful data within 30 days. Multi-specialty hospitals should budget ₹1.5L–₹5L/month across all active campaigns.

**How long does it take for hospital Google Ads to show results?**
First enquiries: Day 1. Meaningful optimisation data: 30–60 days. Fully optimised account at peak efficiency: 90–120 days.

**Should hospitals manage Google Ads in-house or use an agency?**
In-house makes sense if you have a dedicated performance marketer with Google Ads certification and 2+ years of healthcare campaign experience. Otherwise, a specialised healthcare marketing agency will achieve better results faster — the learning curve in healthcare campaign policy and Quality Score optimisation is steep.

**What is a realistic cost per patient for Google Ads in India?**
Based on our client accounts: ₹128–300 per qualified OPD enquiry for well-managed campaigns. For surgical procedures: ₹400–800 per qualified enquiry. These costs typically yield 4–10× ROI when matched against average OPD revenue and downstream procedure conversion.

Google Ads for hospitals in India works — when campaigns are structured by specialty, landing pages are built for conversion, Quality Scores are actively managed, and calls are tracked from click to confirmed appointment. It does not work when treated as a generic digital advertising platform.
